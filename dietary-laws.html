How the guide marks each food

  1. ForbiddenUnclean by the biblical signs or named outright. Practices the Law forbids (blood, carcasses, certain fats) are marked Prohibited.
  2. AvoidAn everyday ingredient made from an unclean source.
  3. VerifyThe source can be clean or unclean. Check the label or ask the maker.
  4. AllowedPermitted by the biblical signs and category markers.
