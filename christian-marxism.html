The question behind the book

Calling a nation Christian does not prove its practices are just.

Christian Marxism examines the relationship between religious institutions, economic power, and national identity—and asks how the language of righteousness can be used to justify exploitation.

Moving through questions of biblical identity, empire, capitalism, and conscience, this book challenges readers to examine the assumptions behind the stories a nation tells about itself.

What do we accept as truth? Whose interests do those beliefs serve? And what does justice require of us?
