Strong Description

Genesis 1 is not treated as a loose religious poem or as a container for later scientific assumptions. This guide studies the chapter as written: The Most High creates, orders, divides, names, fills, blesses, and gives dominion. It distinguishes the created authority given to man from the self-existent supremacy of The Most High, while respecting passages such as Psalm 82 where humans and Israelites can be addressed as "gods" in a delegated judicial sense.

The study also handles common interpretive errors including anachronism, etymological fallacy, context neglect, and illegitimate totality transfer. When Israel is discussed, the guide distinguishes the biblical ethnic Israelites from the modern State of Israel wherever the term could be ambiguous.
