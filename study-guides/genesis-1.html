Genesis 1, Read on Its Own Terms

Genesis 1 must first be read according to its own language, structure, literary context, and biblical cross-references. This guide does not force later scientific, philosophical, denominational, or cultural assumptions back into the chapter. It follows the text as The Most High creates, orders, divides, names, fills, blesses, and gives dominion.

The study argues for seven consecutive 24-hour creation periods and rejects treating the creation days as vast geological ages. It presents mature creation as an interpretive and theological argument, distinguishes man's created dependence from the self-existent supremacy of The Most High, and respects Psalm 82 where appointed rulers or Israelites may be called "gods" in a delegated or judicial sense.

It also guards against anachronism, etymological fallacy, context neglect, and illegitimate totality transfer. Where "Israel" could be ambiguous, biblical ethnic Israel is not automatically conflated with the modern State of Israel.
